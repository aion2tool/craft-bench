      How crafting works

      1. Regular craft. Uses the materials shown at a crafting bench. It can fail below 100% success; the chance rises with your proficiency in that profession. (Planner assumes a failed craft uses up its materials.)
      2. Combo (25%). When a gear craft succeeds, it has a 25% chance to come out as the Splendent version instead, for free.
      3. Splendent upgrade. For Dragon Lord gear you can also turn the normal piece into the Splendent one with a smaller recipe. It always succeeds. If your combo already hit, skip it.
      4. Transfer Material. The previous piece goes into the next recipe. Its enhancement, amplification and Soul Binding carry over, and the result is soul-bound to you.
      5. Combo only. The lower Splendent pieces (e.g. Artisan's Splendent) have no upgrade recipe: you craft the normal one until the combo hits. That's 4 crafts on average; 9 crafts cover 90% of cases.
      6. Safe / Average / Lucky. Safe = no combos hit (plan for this). Average = what it costs over many tries. Lucky = every combo hits.
